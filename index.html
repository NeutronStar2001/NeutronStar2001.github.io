I'm a graduate student at Purple Mountain Observatory(PMO). My research focuses on galaxy formation and evolution, particularly the dynamics and impacts of galaxy mergers.

The effects of merger orbital configurations on reshaping galaxy structures

By using IllustrisTNG-100 and adopting the novel decomposition method of galaxy structure developed by Zhu et al.(2020), we perform a systematic analysis of how the merger orbital configuration influences the structural formation of remnant galaxies. Our analysis reveals that mergers with spiral-in orbits can lead to more aligned orbital planes, i.e. the orbit of the satellites exhibit stronger alignment with the main axis of the host galaxy, which lead to higher fractions of disk/warm components and lower fractions of bulge/hot inner stellar halo components in the remnant galaxy.
